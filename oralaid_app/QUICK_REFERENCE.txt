═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🎯 APPLICATION FLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. User uploads oral cavity image
2. Enter patient details (name, age, gender, history)
3. Click "Analyze Image"
4. AI processes image (MobileNetV3)
5. Returns:
   - Prediction class
   - Confidence score
   - Risk level
   - LIME explanation
   - GradCAM heatmap
6. Results saved to database
7. Display on frontend
