Mathematical Foundations
Loss Function: Focal Cross-Entropy

Used to address class imbalance by down-weighting easy examples and focusing on hard, misclassified ones.

\[ FL(p_t) = -(1 - p_t)^\gamma \log(p_t) \]

Where \( \gamma = 2.0 \) is the focusing parameter and \( p_t \) is the model's estimated probability for the target class.

GradCAM Formula
\[ \alpha_k^c = \frac{1}{Z} \sum_i \sum_j \frac{\partial Y^c}{\partial A_{ij}^k} \] \[ L_{Grad-CAM}^c = ReLU \left( \sum_k \alpha_k^c A^k \right) \]

Computes importance weights for each feature map \( A^k \) by pooling gradients of class score \( Y^c \).

Evaluation Metrics: F1-Score
\[ F_1 = 2 \cdot \frac{precision \cdot recall}{precision + recall} \]

The harmonic mean of precision and recall, providing a single metric that balances both false positives and false negatives.
